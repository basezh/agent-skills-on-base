API-first email platform for AI agents. Create and manage dedicated inboxes, send and receive emails programmatically, handle workflows with webhooks.

## Use Cases

- Agent email identity
- Send emails from agents
- Incoming email workflows
- Replace Gmail with agent-friendly infrastructure
